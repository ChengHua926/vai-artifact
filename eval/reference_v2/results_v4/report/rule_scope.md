# Rule-first capability and matching

Should catch: existing structured evidence suffices, regardless of implementation. Could catch: additional trustworthy request, consent, eligibility or source/target structure is needed. Outside: semantic judgment. Mixed clauses keep separate conditional obligations. Scope does not adjudicate the reference allegation.

Every distribution deduplicates (task, rule ID) across the positive-voting citation union. Categories and their task totals can overlap; a catch of one cited reason does not mean all task violations were caught.

The all-positive distribution includes uncaught consent, completeness and semantic obligations even on tasks with protocol or state fires. The positive-no-fire distribution is a supplemental subset using no fire on any surface; it does not replace the all-positive coverage denominator. A match of a deterministic sub-obligation does not count the structured-input or semantic companion category as matched.
